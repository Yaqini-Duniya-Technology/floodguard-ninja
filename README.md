> **Protecting lives, communities, and livelihoods with AI-powered flood intelligence.**

# 🌍 About FloodGuard AI

Flooding is one of Nigeria's recurring environmental and humanitarian challenges. Heavy rainfall, overflowing rivers, poor drainage systems, urban development, and other environmental factors can expose communities to serious flood risks.

One of the major challenges during flood events is not only the flood itself, but also **access to timely, understandable, and actionable information**.

FloodGuard AI was designed to address this challenge.

The platform provides a centralized digital environment where users can:

* Understand flood risks.
* Check flood-risk information.
* Ask an AI assistant questions about flooding.
* Receive emergency safety guidance.
* Learn what to do before, during, and after a flood.
* Access information through multiple communication channels.
* Find information relevant to different Nigerian states.
* Support faster community awareness and preparedness.

The vision is to make flood intelligence **accessible, understandable, and actionable for Nigerians.**

---

# ⚠️ The Problem

Flooding can cause:

* Loss of lives.
* Destruction of homes.
* Damage to roads and infrastructure.
* Loss of crops and livestock.
* Displacement of families.
* Business interruptions.
* Contamination of water sources.
* Transportation difficulties.
* Economic losses.
* Increased emergency-response pressure.

However, many people may not know:

* Whether their location is at risk.
* What the current flood situation means.
* What actions they should take.
* Where to seek emergency assistance.
* How to access information when internet connectivity is poor.
* How to get information if they do not own a smartphone.

This creates an information gap between **flood risk intelligence and the people who need it.**

---

# 💡 Our Solution

FloodGuard AI provides a digital flood-safety ecosystem designed around **early awareness, accessibility, and emergency preparedness.**

The platform combines:

**AI + Flood Risk Intelligence + Emergency Guidance + Multiple Communication Channels**

For viewing flood-risk information and supporting situational awareness.

# ✨ Key Features

## 🌊 1. Flood Risk Awareness

FloodGuard AI presents flood-risk information in a way that is easier for users to understand.

The platform is designed to help users identify areas experiencing or potentially experiencing higher flood risks.

---

## 🗺️ 2. National Flood Risk Dashboard

The platform includes an emergency dashboard designed around a **Nigeria-wide flood-risk view**.

The dashboard provides a visual representation of flood-risk conditions across Nigerian states.

Users can use the dashboard to understand:

* Areas with higher risk.
* Areas with lower risk.
* Regional differences.
* Potential emergency hotspots.
* General flood-risk patterns across Nigeria.

The national approach is important because flooding does not affect only one city or one state.

---

## 🤖 3. FloodGuard AI Assistant

The platform includes an AI-powered conversational assistant.

The assistant is designed to answer questions related to:

* Flood safety.
* Flood preparedness.
* Emergency actions.
* Evacuation guidance.
* Flood-risk awareness.
* What to do before flooding.
* What to do during flooding.
* What to do after flooding.

The assistant is designed to work with questions relating to **different parts of Nigeria**, rather than being restricted to a single location.

### Example

A user could ask:

> "Is flooding dangerous in my area?"

Or:

> "What should I do if water enters my house?"

Or:

> "How can I prepare my family for flooding?"

The AI assistant then provides practical guidance based on the context of the question.

# 📱 4. SMS Access

Not every Nigerian has reliable smartphone or internet access.

FloodGuard AI therefore includes an SMS-based access concept.

This allows flood-related information and alerts to reach users through basic mobile phones.

Potential SMS capabilities include:

* Flood warnings.

* Location-based risk messages.

This approach helps reduce the digital divide during emergencies.

This would make flood information accessible even without an internet connection.

# 🚨 7. Emergency Guidance

FloodGuard AI focuses not only on prediction but also on **what users should do with the information.**

The platform provides safety guidance covering:

### Before a Flood

Users can be encouraged to:

* Monitor flood warnings.
* Prepare emergency supplies.
* Protect important documents.
* Move valuable items to safer locations.
* Identify evacuation routes.
* Keep phones charged.
* Prepare for possible displacement.

### During a Flood

Users can be guided to:

* Move to higher ground.
* Avoid walking or driving through floodwater.
* Stay away from electrical infrastructure.
* Follow official emergency instructions.
* Avoid contaminated water.
* Assist vulnerable people where it is safe to do so.

### After a Flood

Users can be advised to:

* Avoid unstable structures.
* Avoid contaminated water.
* Stay away from damaged electrical systems.
* Follow official instructions before returning home.
* Report dangerous conditions.
* Seek medical attention when necessary.

FloodGuard AI is currently an **MVP/prototype** created to demonstrate an AI-powered flood-risk awareness and emergency-response concept.
